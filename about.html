About us

A research institute devoted entirely to the brain.

Our physicians work at the frontier of neurology — testing the therapies that become tomorrow's standard of care, right here in West Palm Beach.

Our story

Where the best care and the next breakthrough share one roof.

Premiere Research Institute is built on a simple conviction held by our neurologists: that the patients they treat every day deserve a real seat at the frontier of medicine. Under the direction of Dr. Paul Winner, our team conducts clinical trials for the neurological conditions that affect the most families — Alzheimer's and memory loss, Parkinson's disease, migraine, and multiple sclerosis.

Every study we run is FDA-regulated and reviewed by an independent ethics board before it begins. Participants are cared for as patients first and volunteers second — monitored closely by board-certified neurologists, informed fully at every step, and never charged for study-related care. It's research with a human center.

We're proud to do this work in one place, close to home, alongside the Palm Beach Memory Disorder Center and the Palm Beach Headache Center — so that expert care and emerging science are never more than one visit apart.

Why participate

More than a trial. A different kind of care.

Clinical research offers something standard appointments often can't: time, attention, and access to what medicine is about to become.

01

Emerging treatments, early

Access promising therapies years before they're widely available — when other options are limited, a trial can open a new door.

02

Care at no cost

Study-related exams, testing, and investigational treatment are provided at no charge. Health insurance is not required to take part.

03

Expert, close monitoring

Board-certified neurologists and research staff follow your health closely throughout the study — often more frequently than routine care.

04

Compensation for your time

Many studies compensate participants for time and travel, recognizing the commitment that research participation asks of you.

05

Advancing the science

Your participation helps bring new treatments to everyone who will need them — a contribution that outlasts the study itself.

06

Always your choice

Participation is fully voluntary and fully informed. You may ask questions at any time and withdraw whenever you wish, without affecting your regular care.

The process

From first call to first visit — simple, and at your pace.

Reaching out doesn't commit you to anything. It simply starts a conversation with our research team about whether a study might be right for you.

Start the conversation
  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call us or complete the short form. Tell us a little about the condition and symptoms you're facing — there's no cost and no obligation.

  2. 2

    Pre-screening

    Our coordinators review current studies and ask a few questions by phone to see which trials you may be a fit for.

  3. 3

    Screening visit

    If a study looks like a match, you'll meet our team, review the details fully, and complete a screening evaluation — questions welcome at every step.

  4. 4

    Participate & be cared for

    Take part with close, ongoing monitoring from neurologists and research staff who know you and your condition throughout the study.
