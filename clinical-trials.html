Current clinical trials

Neurological studies now enrolling.

Joining a neurological clinical trial can open the door to new treatments, expert care, and close monitoring when standard options feel limited — while helping advance research that may improve care for others.

What we study

Four conditions, one relentless focus.

Our active and upcoming studies concentrate on the neurological conditions that touch the most families in Palm Beach County. Every trial is FDA-regulated, reviewed by an independent ethics board, and led by board-certified neurologists — with study-related care provided at no cost to participants.

Now enrolling

Alzheimer's & Memory Loss

From mild cognitive impairment through moderate Alzheimer's disease, our memory studies evaluate the next generation of disease-modifying and symptom-focused therapies — including treatments aimed at the changes in thinking, memory, and behavior that families notice most.

Studies here may be right for:

  • Adults experiencing memory changes or a diagnosis of MCI or Alzheimer's
  • Caregivers seeking new options when standard treatments feel limited
  • Those interested in emerging disease-modifying therapies

Parkinson's Disease

Our movement-disorder studies focus on the symptoms that shape daily life with Parkinson's — motor fluctuations and "off" time, tremor and stiffness, and the mood, sleep, and cognitive changes that can accompany the disease as it progresses.

Studies here may be right for:

  • Adults living with Parkinson's disease at various stages
  • People experiencing "off" time or symptom fluctuations
  • Those exploring therapies beyond their current regimen

Migraine

We conduct trials for both the acute treatment of migraine attacks and the prevention of chronic and episodic migraine — evaluating next-generation therapies designed to reduce how often attacks strike and how much they take from your life.

Studies here may be right for:

  • Adults with frequent episodic or chronic migraine
  • People seeking better prevention or faster acute relief
  • Those whose current treatments aren't enough

Why participate

More than a trial. A different kind of care.

Clinical research offers something standard appointments often can't: time, attention, and access to what medicine is about to become.

01

Emerging treatments, early

Access promising therapies years before they're widely available — when other options are limited, a trial can open a new door.

02

Care at no cost

Study-related exams, testing, and investigational treatment are provided at no charge. Health insurance is not required to take part.

03

Expert, close monitoring

Board-certified neurologists and research staff follow your health closely throughout the study — often more frequently than routine care.

04

Compensation for your time

Many studies compensate participants for time and travel, recognizing the commitment that research participation asks of you.

05

Advancing the science

Your participation helps bring new treatments to everyone who will need them — a contribution that outlasts the study itself.

06

Always your choice

Participation is fully voluntary and fully informed. You may ask questions at any time and withdraw whenever you wish, without affecting your regular care.

The process

From first call to first visit — simple, and at your pace.

Reaching out doesn't commit you to anything. It simply starts a conversation with our research team about whether a study might be right for you.

Start the conversation
  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call us or complete the short form. Tell us a little about the condition and symptoms you're facing — there's no cost and no obligation.

  2. 2

    Pre-screening

    Our coordinators review current studies and ask a few questions by phone to see which trials you may be a fit for.

  3. 3

    Screening visit

    If a study looks like a match, you'll meet our team, review the details fully, and complete a screening evaluation — questions welcome at every step.

  4. 4

    Participate & be cared for

    Take part with close, ongoing monitoring from neurologists and research staff who know you and your condition throughout the study.
