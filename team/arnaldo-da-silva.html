Dr. Arnaldo Da Silva specializes in migraine and headache medicine — an area where new therapies have transformed what's possible for patients in recent years. He works closely with people whose lives are disrupted by frequent or severe headache, helping them explore both prevention and relief.

As part of the Premiere Research Institute team, Dr. Da Silva helps guide acute and preventive migraine studies, bringing emerging treatments within reach for the chronic and episodic patients who need them most.
